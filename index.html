અમારા વિશે About the place

Families come here for a night out of the city, for a one-day picnic with the children, for school trips — and for the big functions, when a whole wedding moves in for the weekend.

Hotel Natraj & Resort is in Delwad village, Mansa taluka, right on the Gandhinagar–Mahudi highway road. The cottages sit around the pool and lawns, with the restaurant, banquet hall and game zones a short walk away.

Gandhinagar's well-known places — Akshardham, Indroda Nature Park, the stepwell at Adalaj — are within driving distance, so it works as a base for a day of sightseeing too.

We don't list prices online. Message or call and the team will tell you current rates and availability.

    પ્રશ્નો Before you come

    Things people ask

    Do I need ID at check-in?

    Yes — please bring a valid government-issued photo ID for check-in.

    Is smoking allowed?

    No. The whole property is non-smoking, rooms included.

    Is there room service?

    Yes, in-room dining is available 24 hours a day, with a shorter menu overnight.

    How can I pay?

    All major credit cards are accepted, and debit cards are fine at check-in.

    I booked through another website. How do I change it?

    Please contact the customer service of the site you booked on — they hold that reservation.

    How much does a room cost?

    Rates change with the season and dates, so we don't publish them. WhatsApp or call and the team will tell you.
